Private local MVP · CV matching · Job discovery · Application preparation · Whitelisted email submission

1. Access and CV

Enter the token from your .env file, then upload your own PDF or Word CV. Nothing is sent externally during CV parsing.

No CV loaded yet.

2. Discover relevant jobs

Configure Adzuna API credentials or public Greenhouse / Lever employer board names in .env. No LinkedIn scraping is performed.

Autopilot safety: OFF by default. To enable automatic sending, set AUTO_APPLY_ENABLED=true, an exact email-recipient allowlist and SMTP credentials in .env, then restart. Automatic sending only considers manually verified, email-application vacancies that pass the score and requirement checks.

3. Import an email-application vacancy

Paste a genuine job advert that explicitly says applications can be sent by email. This is the supported starting point for application submission.

4. Application process

For a vacancy accepting email applications: Prepare → Approve → Send. The service adds your CV as an attachment and uses a factual draft based on your skills. A successful send means your email provider accepted the message, not that an employer received, read or accepted your application.
